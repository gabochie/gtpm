GTPM is a continuously updated intelligence platform that maps Ghana's technology ecosystem and continuously monitors the organizations, founders, products, technologies, institutions, capital, regulations, partnerships and market movements shaping Ghana's digital economy.

- a news website
- a founder directory

> **AI-powered Ghana Technology Competitive Intelligence System.**

The system must answer:

1. Who is building what in Ghana?
2. Who is leading each technology sector?
3. Who is growing?
4. Who is gaining funding?
5. Who is launching products?

7. Who is partnering?
